# Nova Lock Repair – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Nova Lock Repair is a Dubai-based locksmith business providing emergency locksmith assistance, residential and commercial lock services, automotive key solutions, and security installations. The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the company as a professional, accessible, and dependable 24/7 locksmith service.

The design prioritises clear service discovery, immediate contact access, and stronger customer confidence while maintaining the supplied brand colours of dark charcoal green (`#212726`), secondary green (`#2A3735`), and white (`#FFFFFF`).

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Service Presentation
- Organised locksmith services into residential, commercial, and automotive categories.
- Improved service presentation to help visitors identify relevant solutions quickly.
- Made key services, including lock repairs, smart locks, car key programming, and emergency assistance, easier to discover.

### 2. Stronger Contact and Booking Journey
- Improved the visibility of call-to-action elements.
- Prioritised direct telephone contact for customers requiring urgent locksmith assistance.
- Made contact options easier to identify and access, particularly for mobile visitors.

### 3. Improved Trust and Credibility
- Strengthened the presentation of business information and 24/7 availability.
- Improved the visibility of relevant service information and customer reassurance elements.
- Created a clearer foundation for presenting genuine customer reviews, real service photographs, and verified service-area information without relying on unsupported claims.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Identity:** Applied the supplied dark green, charcoal, and white colour palette consistently throughout the homepage.
- **Layout:** Improved content hierarchy and section organisation to make important information easier to scan.
- **User Experience:** Prioritised service discovery and direct contact access to support customers seeking urgent assistance.
- **Consistency:** Established a more cohesive visual presentation across service categories, headings, buttons, and content sections.
- **Responsive Presentation:** Focused on making essential service and contact information accessible across desktop and mobile layouts.

The redesigned homepage gives Nova Lock Repair a clearer service-focused presentation, a more direct customer contact journey, and a stronger foundation for building trust with customers across Dubai.
